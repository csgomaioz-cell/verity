What it does

Six things no chat window can do

VERITY is not a tab you visit. It is a resident on your machine with hands, eyes and a phone line.

Speaks and listens

A real voice with a service register. You give an order out loud and it acts — no typing, no prompt craft.

Drives your desktop

Opens apps, moves and organises files, changes settings, runs commands, fixes what is broken. It looks at the screen, acts, and checks the result.

Uses your own browser

Your real Chrome, already signed in. It can read your dashboards, your mail and your accounts without a single new password.

Phones you

When something matters it rings your mobile and tells you in plain speech. You can answer and talk back.

Delegates to Claude and ChatGPT

Real work goes to a Claude worker or to ChatGPT, in the background, and you get the summary — not the raw wall of text.

Runs standing orders

"Every morning at nine…", "while I'm out…", "keep an eye on…". Scheduled work runs on your PC with nobody there.

Questions

The things people ask first

Does my data leave my computer?

Only what is needed to answer goes to the model provider you chose, on your own account, under their terms. There is no VERITY cloud, no server of ours in the middle, and no copy of your files anywhere else.

What does it cost to run?

Your own model usage, billed to you directly: about €4 to €10 a month for light use, €15 to €35 for heavy use. You can cap it in your provider dashboard, and we set that cap with you on install day. You see every cent of it; we never bill it and we never mark it up.

Is it safe to let it control my PC and browser?

It asks before anything it cannot undo — money, deletions, passwords, sending messages as you. Everything else it just does and reports. The boundaries are configured with you during setup, in writing. There is a full page on this: what it will do, and what it will never do — where it runs, whose key it uses, what it can reach, and the six things it never does under any circumstances.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11, a microphone, Chrome, and an Anthropic or OpenAI account. That is all.

Can I cancel the monthly plan?

Any time, with one month's notice. VERITY keeps working on your machine — you simply stop getting updates, new routines and support.

Can you build something specific for my work?

Yes. Custom routines and integrations are included at the small-team tier and quoted separately below it.
